How the tiers were set

Every weapon gets a power number: the square root of its sustained DPS times its kill speed (300 HP divided by the time to kill, reloads included), both scaled by a range factor. The range factor is the share of damage still landing at 10, 20, 35 and 55 m, weighted 25, 35, 25 and 15 percent, with lobbed shots discounted 15 percent. Tiers are that power divided by the median weapon.

Not counted: explosive splash, headshots, accuracy beyond spread, price. Weapons that exist to slow or stun (Frost Blaster, Taser, Freeze Gun) fall to the bottom because their damage is the least of what they do. The minigun includes its 2 s spool-up; the Chain Gun is at full spin. The shot check runs once a frame, so at 60 fps the very fast guns (P90, Vector, minigun) fire about a third slower than the table says.

Recoil: the aim kick per shot, how far it climbs while you hold the trigger, and the screen-kick strength, all from the game's own recoil profile. The bars rate the larger of the held-fire rise and the single-shot kick.
