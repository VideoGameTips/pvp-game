First Steps

Make an account on the login screen (username + password — auto-saves locally so you only do this once). You'll start with 500 credits and the free starter loadout: AK20, SG-8, Pistol, Flare, Fists, Frying Pan, Frag, Medkit. From the mode-select screen you can shop, redeem promo codes, or jump straight into a match.

Special Modes

  • D-Day — Defend the hill against waves; MG42 + AK20 forced.
  • Frontlines — Push the battle line to the enemy base.
  • Last Stand — Survive 10 escalating waves.
  • Shooting Range — Practice mode, no enemies.
  • 👑 King of the Hill (KOTH) — 10 players, 3 lives each. Plays on the 250×250 BR arena map with vehicles, helicopters, and mortars.

🏛️ Lobby System

Every multiplayer mode routes you through a staging lobby. Players ready up, and the undermanned side can vote to fill with bots or play short-handed. Each match runs in its own isolated bubble — concurrent games don't bleed into each other.

📦 Chests & 🧩 Fragments

Open chests to earn weapon fragments. Spend 100 fragments to unlock any priced weapon (alternative to credits), or spend them on upgrades.

ChestCostDrops
Common120💰10-25 frags + 0-30 credits
Rare400💰35-80 frags + 30-100 credits + 5% chance of a free weapon

Match-end drops: Win = 50% common + 15% rare. Loss = 30% common + 5% rare. (Independent rolls — not guaranteed every match.)

✨ Weapon Upgrades

Each owned weapon has three stats, each independently upgradeable up to level 10:

StatPer-Level EffectAt Max (lvl 10)
Damage+12%+120%
Magazine+25%+250% (3.5×)
Reload×0.85-85% (6.7× faster)

Costs per level (per stat): 30 / 60 / 120 / 240 / 480 / 800 / 1,200 / 1,800 / 2,500 / 3,500 fragments. Maxing one stat = 10,710 frags. Maxing all three = 32,130 frags.

🪖 Admin Pass

300 credits for a 10-minute window where every weapon (including all admin items) is equippable. Trial-style flex — once it expires, you go back to what you actually own.

💡 Tips & Tricks

Useful

The default loadout is more versatile than it looks.
Aiming down sights tightens spread on every gun.
Reloading early loses your reserve bullets — finish the mag.
Free spin resets daily at midnight UTC.

Strategic

Cycler never reloads — perfect for finishing weak enemies.
SR-X one-shots headshots.
Stim Shot is faster than Medkit.
Knife users move 2× as fast.
RPD never reloads either.
KOTH gives 3 lives — don't rush the center first.
